Keepsake Instant Film

A camera for moments worth keeping.

Keepsake turns one iPhone photo into an instant-film keepsake. Watch it develop, write on the border, leave a subtle touch behind, and keep the moment.

Need help?

For support, questions, or feedback, email zhangcheng1997@gmail.com.

Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened.

Using Keepsake

  • Allow camera access when you want to take a picture.
  • Take a photo and let the print develop.
  • Optionally write a short caption on the white border.
  • Gently tap the photograph to leave subtle film marks.
  • Tap Keep to save the finished print to Photos, or use Share to send it elsewhere.

Privacy

Keepsake processes photographs on your device. It does not require an account, show ads, or use analytics. Read the full Privacy Policy.
