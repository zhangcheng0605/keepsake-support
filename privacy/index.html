Keepsake Instant Film

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 3, 2026

Keepsake Instant Film (“Keepsake”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) is designed to make an instant-film style photograph on your iPhone. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Summary

Keepsake does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal information or your photos. Photos are processed on your device.

Camera

Keepsake requests access to your camera only so you can take a photograph. The captured image is used on your device to create your instant-film print. We do not upload camera images to our servers because Keepsake does not operate image-processing servers.

Photos library

If you tap Keep, Keepsake requests permission to add the completed print to your Photos library. This permission is used only to save the print you choose to keep. Keepsake does not read, browse, or upload your existing photo library.

Captions and film marks

Captions and subtle film marks are applied locally to the print. They are included in the image only when you choose to save or share it.

Sharing

If you use the iOS Share sheet, you choose the recipient or service. The privacy practices of that recipient or service are governed by its own privacy policy. Keepsake does not receive a copy of content you choose to share.

Data collection

Keepsake does not create user accounts and does not use advertising, analytics SDKs, tracking technologies, or in-app purchases in version 1.0. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact zhangcheng1997@gmail.com.
