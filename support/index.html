Common questions

Why does Keepsake need camera access?

Camera access is used only when you choose to take a photo.

Why does Keepsake ask to add to Photos?

The app requests permission only when you tap Keep and choose to save a finished print to your Photos library.

Does Keepsake have an account or subscription?

No. Version 1.0 is free and does not require an account, subscription, or purchase.

How do I add a caption or film marks?

After taking a photo, tap the lower white border to write a short caption. Gently tap the developing photograph to leave subtle film marks. Both are included when you save or share the finished print.
